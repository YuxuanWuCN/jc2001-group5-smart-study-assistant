## 3. Ten Hard Rules for "Removing the AI Feel / Creating the Startup Feel" (mandatory for the whole team)

> The supervisor said "it looks too much like something an AI agent made" - every rule in this section is an antidote to that sentence. Reviewers detect AI writing style with only three tricks: are there trade-offs, are there failures, are there specific numbers.

1. **Every technical choice must state "the rejected alternatives + reason for rejection + cost"**. Writing only "we used X" = unacceptable; "we used X because we evaluated Y and Z, Y was rejected because..., and the cost was..." = acceptable.
2. **Negative results must be reported**. In `final_systematic_report.csv` there are 3 subjects with zero improvement and 2 with negative improvement (US History −1.0 pp, HS Physics −1.3 pp); these are **bonus points** and must not be deleted.
3. **Every number must have a source**. Any number appearing in the body text must be followed by the source file path (e.g. "145 test cases, source: `tests/`"). Fabricated numbers = rewrite immediately.
4. **Banned filler words**: empower, closed loop, ecosystem, all-round, deep integration, significant improvement, "in summary". Delete on sight anywhere in the text.
5. **Figures > tables > text**. Each chapter has at least 2 figures or tables, each with a `Figure N: ...` / `Table N: ...` number + a one-sentence caption + data source.
6. **Write constraints first, then capabilities**. Emphasise "12 weeks / 10 people / zero budget / mandatory offline demo" up front, so that what follows looks reasonable rather than grandiose.
7. **First-person plural + specific commit/week**. "In Week 4 we merged commit `a0b1efd`..." is a hundred times more credible than "the system was developed".
8. **British spelling + short sentences**: organisation / prioritise / behaviour; no sentence longer than 30 words; no Chinese-style long attributive chains.
9. **Cite real literature**: Sommerville (software engineering), Cockburn (use cases), Ries (The Lean Startup), Osterwalder (Business Model Canvas), Nielsen (usability), Es et al. 2023 (RAGAS). At least 25, Harvard format.
10. **Do not paste whole generated paragraphs**. Every paragraph must be confirmed orally by its named author before being written in English - **being able to withstand three follow-up questions at a team meeting without stumbling** is the only acceptance criterion for this section.

## 6. Team Meeting Response Strategy (turn "cannot answer" into "prepared")

1. **24 hours before every team meeting**: each person takes 5 questions from `supervisor_qa_defense_library.md`, writes three-sentence answers, and posts them in the group chat for peer feedback.
