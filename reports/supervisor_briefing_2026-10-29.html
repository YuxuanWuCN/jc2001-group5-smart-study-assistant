7. Six decisions we want your ruling on — 主动把纠结点交给导师：这是最有效的一条“去 AI 感”动作

  1. Appendix A: how honest is “honest”? Our git history is 59% one author and 5 of 9 outlines are late. We intend to record the skew as it is (0% / +X% / −X% per the official template) rather than smooth it.
    请裁决：如实倾斜的档案在评分与“小组工作”认定上会不会被当成不合格？还是平滑过的档案更容易被质疑？我们准备按你说的执行，并让全员签字。
  2. Do negative results earn credit or cost marks? Ch 6.3 will show 7 gains, 3 ties and 2 losses with the bars coloured. Is that the level of critical evaluation you expect, or should the framing stress the mechanism rather than the score change?
    我们倾向如实呈现（§4B）。想确认它在你眼里是“评估能力”，不是“功能失败”。
  3. RAGAS: state “not run”, or re-run a smaller subset offline? The metrics depend on the cloud instance whose credential we rotated. A partial re-run costs an estimated 4–6 hours and would compete with Week C merging.
    请给一个优先级：诚实留空，还是补一小块带保留说明的数字？
  4. Put the credential incident inside the assessed report? It is verifiable and human. We are unsure whether describing a security mistake in a submitted artefact reads as reflection (good) or as an admission that should be handled privately with the coordinator instead.
    若可以写，我们会限制在 Ch 5.6 半页：事件、检测、补救、复查；不写凭证本身、不写平台名。
  5. How explicit should the AI-use declaration be? Our process used spec-kit and multi-agent scaffolding for code and drafting. Every submission passes Turnitin and we follow University academic-integrity guidance. We want the declaration to match how the marker actually reads it.
    请给一个可操作边界：写“工具清单 + 人的决策清单”够不够，还是需要逐章标注？
  6. How much business framing is safe? The brief asks for intended users and practical/business value, so Ch 1.6 and 7.3 include three pricing scenarios marked as assumptions. Where is the point at which that reads like invented market claims?
    我们目前的边界：不编用户调研（没有 n=200 问卷这种东西），只写文献、竞品分析和组内 10 人的非正式验证。
