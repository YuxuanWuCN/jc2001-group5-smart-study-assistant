## 1. Project Title
**To Design and Implement a Smart Study Assistant System for University Students**

---

## 2. Problem
As university students manage complex course loads across multiple subjects, the manual organization of study materials, progress tracking, and revision scheduling poses significant efficiency and productivity challenges. Students frequently struggle with disorganized notes, difficulty in assessing their knowledge retention before exams, and inconsistent study habits. Inconsistency in learning approaches and lack of structured feedback are major risks that lead to lower learning productivity and academic stress.

---

## 3. Solution
We propose the development of a **Smart Study Assistant System** that automates and streamlines the current approach to learning material management, study schedule tracking, interactive self-assessment, and progress visualization. The system enables students to organize course topics efficiently, generate structured practice quizzes, and track their study retention through an intuitive user interface, ensuring accuracy, continuity, and effectiveness in their personal learning processes.

---

## 4. Objectives
With this project, we intend to achieve the following specific and achievable goals:
* **(a)** Design, develop, and deploy a functional Proof-of-Concept (PoC) Smart Study Assistant software system on or before **14 December 2026**.
* **(b)** Implement core study management features, including topic organization, automated review tracking, and self-testing mechanisms.
* **(c)** Ensure system usability, security, and data consistency for student study records throughout the project period.

---

## 5. Benefits
The proposed Smart Study Assistant System will help students maintain consistency in their study routines and significantly reduce material organization overhead. By automating learning progress tracking and revision scheduling, users obtain long-term sustainability benefits in their academic productivity and self-regulated learning habits.
